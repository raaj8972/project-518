## 1. Royal Gallery

Image: [01-royal-gallery.png](./01-royal-gallery.png)

Final generation prompt:

```text
Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: high fidelity sponsor webpage design concept for the existing RICORRENZA 2026 cultural festival at IQ City Medical College.
Create ONE beautifully art-directed, realistic desktop website screenshot, flat front view, portrait 3:4 canvas showing a complete polished webpage from navigation through partnership call-to-action. It should look like an exceptional professionally designed website with real implementable cards, not a poster or a wireframe.
EXISTING THEME MUST STAY: cinematic emerald ruined city with aged-gold architectural rings, dark green stone, restrained green magical light, wisps of mist, a little fine gold dust. Backgrounds #050d08 and #0b1b12, aged-gold #d9ba6c edges, cream #f4efd9 copy, small green #96f587 highlights. Gold italic Playfair Display-style large serif headings with crisp Poppins-style smaller sans-serif copy. Preserve this visual identity throughout. No purple, blue, pink or red; no alternate light theme.
At the top keep a narrow elegant dark green beveled navigation bar with fine aged-gold edge, RICORRENZA 2026 wordmark, short links Home, Events, Sponsors, Gallery, and Login; Sponsors active. Site content should feel spacious and curated, strong clear hierarchy, highly refined lighting and shadows, exact alignments, generous padding, readable labels. Keep the dramatic architecture around edges and in hero, never across readable text. Big beautiful sponsor cards are the primary focus, with a generously sized centered logo display, tier label, partner-name caption and small Visit website arrow. Give logos real empty breathing room. Placeholder marks are stylized A, B, C monograms, labeled Partner name; they represent sample brands only. No real external sponsor logos or invented prices, attendance figures, testimonials or metrics.
Required page content: hero heading "Our Partners" with small "RICORRENZA 2026 / IQ CITY" eyebrow and one short subtitle "The brands behind the celebration."; a prominent "Title Sponsor" feature; "Co-Sponsors"; smaller "Official Partners" cards; bottom invitation "Be part of the celebration" with "Become a sponsor" and "Download brochure" buttons and a discreet footer. Keep text short and legible. Do not add website code, design annotations, device frames, perspective distortions or a collage. Create an actual attractive finished page concept.

DESIGN DIRECTION 01 — ROYAL GALLERY.
A majestic symmetric gallery of emerald and aged-gold. Compact cinematic hero occupies top quarter, with ruined stone arches at extreme left and right fading into dark background, big centered gold italic Our Partners. Under it a centered fine-line label TITLE SPONSOR above ONE wide spectacular centerpiece sponsor card, about 75% page width and 230px in proportional height. This card has an elegant stepped gold double frame with restrained beveled corners, dark brushed emerald surface and a big central gold A monogram, title sponsor badge at top, Partner name and arrow below. Soft warm light on upper corners creates dimensionality without overwhelming border glow. Under it place two equal gorgeous co-sponsor cards side by side, then a refined row of four smaller square official-partner cards. Clear category labels and gold hairline ornaments. Keep all sponsor logos and names large. Bottom invitation in a low wide green-gold panel. Sophisticated luxurious cinematic festival website, highly intentional hierarchy and fine material detail, not overly ornate.
```
