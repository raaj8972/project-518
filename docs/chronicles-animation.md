# Chronicles animation

The energy wand follows the measured SVG from the valley upward through every published card. Each card holds the wand for 3.2 seconds and fades its illumination in and out. The character's eyes stay dark during the journey, glow only after arrival, then fade during a 5.2-second eye phase. A 3.4-second dark rest separates cycles. Travel duration follows path length and viewport width; adding years adds travel and dwell time automatically.

The background orbit rotates independently. Animation pauses outside the viewport and in hidden tabs. Reduced motion keeps the character's eyes dark and stops the moving effects.
