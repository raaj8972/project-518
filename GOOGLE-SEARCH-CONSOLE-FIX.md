## Why Google Search Console Still Shows Old Data:

### 1. **Google's Cache is Outdated** 💾
Google has cached the old version with "2025". They haven't re-crawled yet.

**Timeline:** It can take 2-7 days (sometimes up to 2 weeks) for Google to naturally re-crawl.

### 2. **Indexing Delay** ⏰
Even when Googlebot crawls your site, it takes time to:
1. Process the new content
2. Update their index
3. Show in Search Console

## 🚀 How to Fix It FASTER:

### Option 1: Request Manual Re-Indexing (Recommended)

1. **Go to Google Search Console:**
   - Visit: https://search.google.com/search-console

2. **Use URL Inspection Tool:**
   - Click "URL Inspection" in left sidebar
   - Enter: `https://ricorrenza.fun/`
   - Click "Request Indexing"

3. **Submit Your Sitemap:**
   - Go to "Sitemaps" in left sidebar
   - Submit: `https://ricorrenza.fun/sitemap.xml`
   - This tells Google to re-crawl all pages

### Option 2: Generate Updated Sitemap

If you have a sitemap.xml file, make sure it's updated and submit it to Google.

### Option 3: Increase Crawl Rate (Advanced)

1. In Google Search Console
2. Go to Settings → Crawl Rate
3. Submit multiple URLs for re-indexing

## Common Questions:

**Q: How long will it take?**
A: Usually 2-7 days after requesting indexing. Search results might take longer to update.

**Q: Why is it so slow?**
A: Google processes billions of pages. They prioritize based on site authority, crawl budget, and update frequency.

**Q: Can I speed it up more?**
A: Not really. Requesting indexing is the fastest option. The rest is up to Google's algorithms.

**Q: Will old search results still show 2025?**
A: Yes, for a while. Google's cache updates gradually. Old snippets can persist for weeks.
